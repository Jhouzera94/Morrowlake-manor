Absolutely. Since you’re at roughly 400+ followers, I’d make the public goal part of the project:

“Building Manners + growing this page from 400 → 1,000 before the game is finished.”

That gives people two journeys to follow: the game and the account.

8-week Manners content plan
Week	Manners focus	What to post
1 — Introduce	Explain the project	Reel: “I’m making a game called Manners…” • Carousel: concept/art/screenshots • Stories: “Ask me anything about the game”
2 — The idea	Why Manners exists	Reel explaining the original idea • Old vs current version • Poll about a design decision
3 — Building	Show development	2–3 short gameplay clips • A bug/funny failure • “Today I’m working on…” Story
4 — Mechanic	Highlight one interesting mechanic	Reel demonstrating it • Before/after improvement • Ask followers what they would change
5 — World/visuals	Make it visually recognizable	Environment/art Reel • Timelapse • Carousel showing details people might miss
6 — Personality	Put you into the account	“What making Manners actually looks like” • Mistakes/frustrations • A small development win
7 — Community	Let followers participate	Poll between two options • Ask for ideas/names • Implement one follower suggestion and tag them
8 — Milestone	Show how far you've come	“Manners 8 weeks ago vs today” • Progress montage • Announce the next big milestone/playtest

You don't need finished-looking material. The unfinished stuff is actually your content. A weird bug, ugly prototype, animation test, menu redesign, sound experiment, sketch, failed mechanic—all of those can become posts.

I'd aim for 3 strong Reels per week, rather than exhausting yourself trying to post every day. Stories can be much more casual.

And give yourself three recurring Reel formats:

1. “Building Manners until it's finished — Day/Week X”
Show one visually interesting thing you did. Keep it around 10–25 seconds.

2. “Making a game is basically…”
The funny/relatable format: bugs, things breaking, spending three hours fixing something tiny, etc. These have more potential to reach people who don't already follow you.

3. “You decide what goes into Manners.”
Give viewers A/B choices. Then actually show the result in another Reel. That creates a reason to follow.

Your first week could literally be

Monday: Reel — “I'm making Manners, and I'm trying to reach 1,000 followers before I finish it.”

Wednesday: Reel — show the most visually interesting thing that already works. Don't explain everything. Hook with something like: “I've been working on this mechanic for Manners…”

Friday: Reel — something that went wrong. Bug, failed experiment, ridiculous development moment.

Throughout the week: Stories showing tiny progress updates, screenshots and polls. They don't need editing.

Most importantly, don't make every post “here's an update on my game.” Someone who has never heard of Manners has no reason to care yet.

Instead, make each Reel contain its own little story, question, surprise, joke, transformation, or payoff. The game is the subject, but the content still needs to entertain someone encountering you for the first time.
