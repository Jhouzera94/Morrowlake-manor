# Morrowlake Manor

## The Night the House Stopped

### 14 October 1996 — 2:41 AM

The player wakes on the floor of an unfamiliar bedroom.

Rain strikes the windows. Wind moves through cracks in the walls. The wallpaper is peeling, furniture is covered in dust, and parts of the ceiling have collapsed.

Beside the player stands a strange machine.

Its metal frame is burned. Wires hang from its sides. A large circular mechanism dominates its centre, but there is no power.

The player doesn't know who they are or why they are inside Morrowlake Manor.

On a table beside the machine is an old tape recorder.

The player presses **PLAY**.

A man's frightened voice speaks through the static:

*"If you woke up, the machine failed."*

A pause.

*"Restore the power. Find my plans. And whatever happens..."*

The recording distorts.

*"Do not trust what the house tells you."*

The tape ends.

---

## The Abandoned Manor

The player begins exploring.

Morrowlake Manor has been abandoned for decades.

But the building itself tells the player that something terrible happened here.

One hallway is blackened by fire.

A bedroom has been completely sealed.

A grandfather clock has stopped at **11:47 PM**.

Portraits have been removed from the walls, leaving pale rectangles where they once hung.

A dining table remains set for several people, but everything is covered in seventy years of dust.

And throughout the manor are strange cables running through walls that are far older than electrical wiring should be.

The player eventually discovers a blueprint.

It belongs to **Dr. Elias Morrowlake**, the former owner of the manor.

The blueprint reveals something strange.

The architecture shown on the plans doesn't completely match the house.

There are rooms that shouldn't exist.

Hidden passages.

Electrical conduits running beneath the floors.

And a line connecting the manor to an old tower on the estate.

Written across the blueprint:

**TEMPORAL TRANSMISSION SYSTEM — MORROWLAKE PROJECT**

The player now understands that the broken machine upstairs isn't simply a machine.

It is a time machine.

---

## Restoring the Manor

The blueprint leads the player toward a locked section of the house.

Exploration leads to a manor key.

The key opens a room containing more of Morrowlake's research.

His notes reveal that the manor itself was part of the experiment.

The rooms, electrical system and tower were constructed around the machine.

The house was effectively one enormous circuit.

But the generator outside is dead.

The player follows the blueprint through a hidden route and reaches the generator house.

A fuse is missing.

After searching the estate and recovering it, the player installs the fuse.

The generator starts.

For the first time in decades, Morrowlake Manor comes alive.

Lights flicker through the corridors.

Electrical systems begin humming behind the walls.

Previously inaccessible doors unlock.

Machines begin operating.

And somewhere above the manor, the tower activates.

But something else happens.

The player begins hearing things.

Footsteps upstairs.

A telephone ringing in an empty room.

Music playing behind a wall.

Voices coming from rooms that contain nobody.

The house seems to remember.

---

## The Machine

With electricity restored, the player can finally reach the tower connection.

There they discover the final component required to repair the time machine.

But beside it is another recording from Elias Morrowlake.

His voice is different this time.

Calmer.

Older.

*"If you're hearing this, then I was right."*

*"The machine cannot change where something exists."*

*"Only when."*

*"Whatever enters the machine returns to the same place... at another moment in time."*

Then he says something disturbing.

*"I know you will come."*

The player returns to the bedroom.

They install the final component.

The machine activates.

The room fills with light.

The player steps inside.

And everything disappears.

---

# 14 October 1926 — 9:18 PM

The player opens their eyes.

Same room.

Same manor.

But everything is different.

The wallpaper is clean.

The furniture is new.

Fire burns in the fireplace.

Electric lights illuminate the hallway.

Music comes from downstairs.

People are inside the house.

Morrowlake Manor is alive.

The player has travelled exactly seventy years into the past.

And they have arrived on the night the house was destroyed.

---

## Seeing the House Again

The player now explores the same architecture for a second time.

But every room means something different.

The abandoned dining room is filled with guests.

The burned hallway is pristine.

The sealed bedroom belongs to Morrowlake's daughter, **Evelyn**.

The empty office contains Elias's research.

The mysterious cables seen in 1996 are currently being installed throughout the manor.

And the tower outside is operating at full power.

The player gradually reconstructs what happened.

Elias Morrowlake was obsessed with time.

Years earlier, his wife had died.

He became convinced that time was not something humanity needed to accept.

If time could be crossed, death itself could theoretically be undone.

He transformed his manor into a laboratory and built the machine in secret.

But his experiments became increasingly dangerous.

His daughter Evelyn begged him to stop.

Elias refused.

Tonight would be the first full test.

---

## The Disaster

As the player explores, they discover something even stranger.

Elias already knows they are here.

Eventually the player enters his study.

Elias is waiting.

He looks at the player and says:

*"You finally came."*

The player doesn't understand.

Elias explains that during earlier experiments he briefly saw fragments of the future.

The manor destroyed.

His research forgotten.

And one person waking beside the machine seventy years later.

The player.

That is why the recordings existed.

They were never instructions left randomly for someone in the future.

They were left specifically for the player.

Elias knew the player would find them because he had already seen it happen.

But there is one thing he never understood.

Why the player was there.

Until now.

The player discovers an old photograph.

It shows Elias.

Evelyn.

And a small child.

The child's face is unmistakable.

It is the player.

The player was born in 1926.

They did not arrive at Morrowlake Manor for the first time in 1996.

They **returned** there.

---

## What Really Happened

On the night of the disaster, the machine became unstable.

Elias realised the temporal field was going to destroy the manor.

There wasn't enough time to shut it down.

Evelyn took the child—the player—and placed them inside the machine.

She sent them into the future to save their life.

The machine transported the child exactly seventy years forward.

But the journey damaged the machine and affected the player's memory.

The player woke in 1996 believing they had simply appeared there.

The abandoned manor they explored was actually the ruin of their childhood home.

The bedroom where they woke was their mother's room.

The sealed room was their own childhood bedroom.

The objects scattered throughout the house weren't simply environmental clues.

They belonged to their family.

---

## The Night the House Stopped

The clock approaches **11:47 PM**.

The player recognises the time.

Every broken clock they saw in 1996 had stopped at exactly this moment.

Now they understand why.

**11:47 PM is when the experiment fails.**

The player has one final decision.

They can follow history.

Allow the disaster to happen.

Enter the machine.

Return to 1996.

And preserve the timeline that led them here.

Or they can try to stop Elias.

Destroy the machine before the experiment begins.

Save Morrowlake Manor.

Save Evelyn.

Save everyone inside.

But doing so creates a terrifying question:

If the disaster never happens...

the player is never sent to 1996.

They never discover the abandoned manor.

They never repair the machine.

And they never travel back to stop the disaster.

The player stands inside the same house they explored as a ruin.

But now every room has meaning.

Every damaged wall they saw in the future represents something that is about to happen.

Every abandoned object belongs to someone who is currently alive.

And somewhere in the manor, a clock continues ticking toward:

**11:47 PM.**

**MORROWLAKE MANOR**

*The Night the House Stopped.*
