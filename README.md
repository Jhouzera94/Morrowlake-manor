# Morrowlake Manor

**Morrowlake Manor** is a first-person mystery and puzzle-solving game centered around exploration, environmental storytelling, machinery, and time travel.

## About the Game

You find yourself trapped inside the mysterious Morrowlake Manor, far from your normal life and in the wrong time.

To find your way back, you must explore the manor, investigate its rooms, study its architecture, discover artifacts, and piece together what happened there.

Progress requires solving puzzles and finding, repairing, and connecting parts of a mysterious machine. These include components such as generators, fuse boxes, a tower, a stabilizer, and a computer.

Your ultimate goal is to restore the machine and find a way back through time.

But Morrowlake Manor isn't just about escaping.

The story is designed to be discovered through the world itself. Objects, artifacts, rooms, architecture, machinery, and environmental details all contain pieces of the manor's history.

Players must observe their surroundings and put those pieces together for themselves.

## Current Development Progress

The current development milestone focuses on the manor’s time-travel sequence.

Implemented or in active testing:

- Restorable manor power system
- Phase Regulator and Energy Tower progression
- Portal cable and handheld-device collection
- Five-step wrench repair interaction
- Destination computer with year-entry interface
- 1950 destination level based on the present-day manor
- Separate 1996 and 1950 atmosphere and lighting
- Tool inventory with wrench and screwdriver selection
- Objective and interaction prompts for the portal sequence

The intended sequence is:

**Restore power → activate the machinery → install the missing portal components → repair the portal → enter 1950 at the destination computer → return to the portal and travel.**

The portal visuals, destination interface, audio feedback, and transition into the 1950 level are currently being tested and polished.
